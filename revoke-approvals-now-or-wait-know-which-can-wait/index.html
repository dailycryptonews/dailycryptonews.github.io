Revoke approvals now or wait? Know which can wait

Revoke approvals now or wait for lower gas fees? Revoke risky spenders immediately; wait on reputable dormant approvals until a quiet fee window.

The Blockheight Editors·Nov 21, 2026 · 07:25 UTC·3 min read

A token approval keeps exposing your wallet until it is revoked, while a fee spike is temporary. So revoke suspicious or compromised spenders now; dormant approvals to reputable contracts can wait for lower fees.

  • Unknown spender with unlimited USDC: revoke now.
  • Old Uniswap router approval: wait for a quiet weekend.
  • On a cheap L2, the fee saving may be too small to justify waiting.

revokecash is a token approval management tool for inspecting and managing the permissions your wallet has granted to smart contracts. Its coverage of revokecash is a place to go further on how to review those approvals and decide which ones to revoke.

What do lower gas fees actually save?

Waiting changes the per-unit gas price, not the gas units the revoke uses. Under EIP-1559 on Ethereum, the fee is gas units multiplied by the base fee plus the priority fee at inclusion, and it is paid in the network’s native token.

A revoke is an approve(spender, 0) call, or setApprovalForAll(operator, false) for an operator. The call’s gas units stay fixed; the network’s base fee can move while you wait. On Arbitrum or Base, where fees can be low, delaying a routine revoke may save too little to matter.

Can waiting put an urgent revoke at risk?

Yes: a pending revoke against an active drainer can lose the race. A drainer watching the mempool may see the revoke and submit transferFrom with a higher fee, trying to have the transfer included first.

That is why an urgent revoke should use a competitive priority fee for current network conditions. A low-fee transaction left pending to save money gives an active spender time to act; the exposure continues until the revoke is included.

Revisit the decision if the spender becomes suspicious, a contract is compromised, or fees fall enough to make routine cleanup cheap. A change in risk calls for immediate revocation; a change in fees can make waiting less useful.

What else should I know about revoke timing?

How do I speed up a stuck revoke transaction?

Resubmit the same transaction with the same nonce and a higher fee, using your wallet’s speed-up option. The replacement must meet the network’s fee rules to replace the pending transaction.

Can a revoke be scheduled automatically?

Some tools can help manage approvals, but automatic revocation depends on the tool and your wallet’s authorization. Review what a scheduled action will revoke before enabling it.

Does revoking on an L2 also clear the same approval on Ethereum?

No. Approvals are recorded separately on each network, so revoke on Ethereum and on each L2 where you granted the approval.
