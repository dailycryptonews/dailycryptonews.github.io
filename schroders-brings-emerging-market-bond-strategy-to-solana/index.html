Schroders brings emerging-market bond strategy to Solana

Schroders has launched tokenized exposure to its emerging-market bond strategy on Solana with Centrifuge, marking its first deployment on the network.

The Blockheight Editors·Nov 7, 2026 · 04:54 UTC·2 min read

Schroders and tokenization firm Centrifuge launched tokenized exposure to Schroders’ emerging-market bond strategy on Solana on Nov. 4, according to Centrifuge’s announcement. The launch marks Schroders’ first deployment on Solana, the asset manager said.

The product is linked to the Schroder International Selection Fund Emerging Market Bond. Schroders said the strategy covers sovereign, corporate and local-currency debt; the firm has more than 40 investment professionals focused on emerging-markets debt and has invested in the asset class for over 50 years.

What do token holders own?

They hold tokenized shares in a feeder fund, not direct shares in Schroders’ underlying fund, CoinDesk reported. The feeder invests in Schroders’ Emerging Market Bond Fund, and its share value tracks the fund’s net asset value, according to CoinDesk.

That structure gives investors tokenized exposure to the strategy through the feeder. It does not mean the underlying bonds themselves are issued as Solana tokens, based on the structure described in the report.

Why is the Solana launch notable?

Schroders said this is its first deployment on the network. Centrifuge said the fund initially launches on Solana, which it described as having more than $4 billion in tokenized real-world assets.

The launch brings an actively managed emerging-markets debt strategy onto a public blockchain. Centrifuge’s announcement characterized the move as an expansion of onchain fixed income beyond cash and short-duration government securities.

What comes next, and what remains undisclosed?

The fund initially launches on Solana, according to Centrifuge. Neither the announcement nor CoinDesk’s report specifies the launch’s total value, token availability or investor eligibility, so those details remain unconfirmed.
