How do you track an Ethereum-to-Solana transfer on Wormholescan?

To track a transfer on Wormholescan, search your source transaction hash and follow it from finality through VAA signing to redemption on the destination chain.

The Blockheight Editors·Nov 28, 2026 · 04:56 UTC·3 min read

To track a transfer on Wormholescan, paste the source transaction hash into its search bar and read the status from source finality through VAA signing to destination completion; a signed VAA can still be waiting for redeem.

Say you sent USDC from Ethereum to Solana through Wormhole Portal. The USDC bridge transfer moves tokens across chains; here, the USDC bridge’s source transaction is the one to search.

Copy the transaction hash from your Ethereum wallet or its transaction history. Choose the matching network in Wormholescan, paste the hash in the search bar and submit. You can also search a wallet address to find its transactions. The result shows the source transaction, current status and VAA ID.

Read the transfer status and VAA ID

The transfer remains pending while the source transaction is reaching finality and Guardians are signing its message. Once the source transaction is final, Wormhole’s 19 Guardians observe it and sign a VAA; 13 signatures make that VAA valid. The status then indicates whether the transfer is signed or has completed on Solana.

The VAA ID contains the emitter chain, emitter address and sequence number. Its chain number is Wormhole’s own chain ID: Solana is 1 and Ethereum is 2. That number identifies the source chain in the VAA; it is not Ethereum’s EVM chain ID. Wormhole’s documentation describes the search fields and VAA ID, and lists Solana and Ethereum’s Wormhole chain IDs.

For this Ethereum-to-Solana Portal transfer, redeem is the final step. After the signed VAA is submitted and the destination transaction completes, the tokens arrive on Solana as USDCet, the Portal-wrapped Ethereum USDC. A signed status means the VAA is ready; look for destination completion to confirm the redeem.

Questions readers ask next

How do I redeem a signed VAA myself?

Return to the bridge or app that started the transfer and use its resume, claim or redeem option. Follow its wallet prompts to submit the VAA on Solana; Wormholescan tracks the transfer but does not itself redeem it.

Why does my transfer show no VAA after an hour?

A VAA may be absent while the source transaction is not final or Guardians have not reached 13 signatures. Check that the source transaction succeeded and that Wormholescan is on the same network, then check the transfer again.

Can I swap USDCet for native USDC?

USDCet is a wrapped token representing USDC bridged from Ethereum through Portal, not native Solana USDC. Whether you can swap it depends on available Solana markets and liquidity.

Does Wormholescan show CCTP transfers too?

Yes, Wormholescan can display Wormhole CCTP transfers. Their attestation and redemption steps differ from the Portal wrapped-token flow described here.
