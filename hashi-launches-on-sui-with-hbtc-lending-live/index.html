Hashi launches on Sui with hBTC lending live

Hashi went live on Sui on Nov. 19, issuing 1:1 hBTC against Bitcoin for collateral use; Fluid opened USDC borrowing, with more integrations planned.

The Blockheight Editors·Nov 23, 2026 · 13:58 UTC·2 min read

Hashi went live on Sui mainnet on Nov. 19, issuing hBTC against Bitcoin held on the Bitcoin network, according to the Sui Foundation’s launch announcement. The token is minted at a 1:1 ratio and can be used as collateral for lending and other onchain products, The Block reported.

The launch gives Bitcoin holders a route to borrow against BTC on Sui without moving the underlying coins off the Bitcoin network, according to Sui. The protocol mints hBTC on Sui, where it can be used in lending markets and vaults.

Fluid is accepting hBTC as collateral for USDC loans, with additional borrowing options expected, The Block reported. Sui said Fluid’s Bitcoin-backed stablecoin lending is live, starting with USDC.

HoneyB opened two vaults on Upshift, one for hBTC and one for USDC, according to The Block. Sui said the strategies may include exposure to real-world assets, alternative products and publicly traded markets. Upshift’s vaults are live, while Sui said Ember’s Hashi vaults are expected later in 2026.

Sui said more than 20 custody, retail and DeFi participants are building products around Hashi. It named Anchorage Digital, Fluid, HoneyB and Upshift among the launch participants. Institutions can access hBTC and selected Fluid strategies through Porto, Anchorage Digital’s self-custody wallet, according to Sui and The Block.

BitGo support and tri-party settlement are due in early 2027, The Block reported. Sui also said further ways to access hBTC and stablecoin strategies are expected through the end of 2026 and into 2027. Neither launch account quantified how much BTC had been deposited or how much hBTC was in circulation at launch.
