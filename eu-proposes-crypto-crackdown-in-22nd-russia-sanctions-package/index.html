EU proposes crypto crackdown in 22nd Russia sanctions package

The European Commission proposed targeting 60 Russian banks and crypto networks in a new Russia sanctions package, but all 27 EU states must approve it.

The Blockheight Editors·Nov 27, 2026 · 10:33 UTC·2 min read

The European Commission proposed new Russia sanctions on Nov. 23, including restrictions on crypto and payment networks used to evade sanctions, according to the Associated Press report. The measures are part of the Commission’s proposed 22nd package and have not taken effect.

Commission President Ursula von der Leyen said the proposal would extend transaction bans to about 60 Russian banks and remove them from the SWIFT international payment messaging system. The Commission also said the crypto measures would cover Russia and third countries that help it evade sanctions, and announced a Crypto Sanctions Task Force.

The Commission’s statement on the package does not name crypto platforms or describe the proposed restrictions on them in detail. It says the task force will support the work, with coordination with international partners also a stated aim.

The package also proposes listing more than 70 ships in Russia’s shadow fleet and banning tanker vessels insured in Russia. In sports, the Commission proposes barring Russian teams and athletes from competing under the Russian flag at events in the EU, restricting Russian sponsors’ financial flows to European sports and cultural associations, and banning official Russian symbols at those events.

The measures still require endorsement by all 27 EU member states before they can enter into force, the AP reported. That process can take weeks, and the proposals may change. The Commission has not published a list of affected crypto platforms in its statement, so the scope and implementation of the crypto restrictions remain unconfirmed.
