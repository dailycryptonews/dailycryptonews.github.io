How to Check a Bridge Transfer to Manta Pacific

Check a Manta Pacific bridge transfer by matching its source transaction, route and wallet address, then confirm the destination credit in History or the network explorer.

The Blockheight Editors·Sep 30, 2026·2 min read

Check a Manta Pacific bridge transfer by matching the source transaction to the destination credit in the bridge’s History view or the network explorer. A source transaction marked successful confirms that step completed; it does not, by itself, confirm that the funds arrived on Manta Pacific.

Which details should I check before bridging?

Check the route, token, amount and destination wallet before you sign, then keep the source transaction hash. Manta’s bridge interface separates deposits, withdrawals and History, so first confirm you used the route that matches your transfer. For a fuller explanation of route choices, read the manta bridge guide; the route matters because different paths can have different steps and status screens.

Compare the address shown in the transfer with the wallet you control, and check the token on the destination network rather than relying on its ticker alone. Manta Pacific is an Ethereum layer 2; Manta’s network information lists chain ID 169 and links to its block explorer.

How do I verify a deposit reached Manta Pacific?

For a deposit, check the source transaction first, then look for the matching arrival on Manta Pacific. Open the transaction hash in the source network’s explorer and confirm its status, token, amount and recipient; then use the bridge’s History view or Manta Pacific’s explorer to check the destination activity.

  • Confirm the source transaction succeeded and is on the network you selected.
  • Compare the token and amount with the bridge quote, allowing for any displayed fee.
  • Check that the destination address matches your wallet.
  • Look for the resulting token balance or transaction on Manta Pacific.

Manta’s bridge page displays a History option, while its network information page identifies the Pacific explorer. If the source transaction succeeded but no destination activity appears, use the bridge history to see whether the transfer is still processing before sending again.

Why can a bridge transfer still be pending?

A transfer can remain pending after its source transaction succeeds because the bridge still has to process the cross-network message. The source chain’s confirmation and the destination credit are separate checks, so a successful source receipt is not the same as a completed transfer.

Withdrawals also follow the route’s return process, which can require additional network steps after the initial transaction. Check the bridge’s status and the relevant explorer for any later action or transaction; avoid repeating a transfer while the first one is unresolved, since that could create a second transfer.

What should I do if the transfer does not appear?

Use the original hash and exact wallet address to trace the transfer, and compare the route and token shown in the bridge with the records on both networks. If the bridge lists an unresolved status, follow its displayed next step; if the source transaction failed, the explorer will show that rather than a destination credit.

Manta says third-party bridge providers linked from its interface operate independently, so their transfer status and support process may differ from Manta’s own bridge. The next check is the route’s current History status; whether a pending transfer needs another action depends on what that record and the destination explorer show.
