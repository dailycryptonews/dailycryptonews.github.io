About Paraswap Dispatch

Paraswap Dispatch is an independent publication covering swap routing, execution economics and developer integrations. We examine transactions, contracts and reproducible measurements to explain how Paraswap works and where its tradeoffs matter.

We are not affiliated with Paraswap. We prioritize primary sources, distinguish measured results from estimates and disclose relevant conflicts. Comparisons identify their sample, timeframe and limitations. We correct material errors visibly and explain what changed. Sponsorship never determines our findings or conclusions.
