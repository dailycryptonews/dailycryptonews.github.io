About Hashmark

Hashmark reports news from across crypto: Bitcoin and Ethereum, layer-2 networks, exchanges, stablecoins, DeFi, security incidents and the regulation that shapes the market. We cover many projects and are tied to none of them.

Every story starts from primary sources such as filings, on-chain records, official statements and direct confirmation, and we say plainly when a claim is unverified. Errors are corrected in the article with a dated note. Hashmark is independent, and nothing published here is financial advice.
