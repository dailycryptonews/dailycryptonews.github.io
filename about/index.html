About Blockheight

Blockheight is an independent newsroom reporting on crypto: Bitcoin, Ethereum and other networks, DeFi, exchanges, stablecoins, regulation and security. Each story is built from primary sources such as filings, on-chain data, project statements and official announcements, and says clearly what is confirmed and what is not.

We take no payment for coverage. When we get something wrong, we correct the article and add a dated note. Blockheight is independent, and nothing we publish is financial advice.
