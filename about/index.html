About Daily Paraswap News

Onchain Market Daily covers decentralized exchanges, swap aggregators and the market infrastructure behind crypto trading. Our reporting follows ParaSwap routing, liquidity, execution quality and protocol economics through verifiable on-chain data.

We operate as an independent newsroom. We prioritize transaction records, governance proposals and primary documentation, distinguish reported facts from estimates, and disclose material conflicts. Claims from projects receive scrutiny rather than automatic endorsement. We correct substantive errors openly and identify what changed in the affected article.
