About Token Hub

Token Hub is an independent newsroom that reports crypto as wire copy: markets, exchanges, protocols, regulation, security incidents and the companies building across the sector. Every report leads with the fact, names its sources and links to primary documents where they exist.

No project we cover owns or pays us. When we get something wrong, we correct it in the article and add a dated note. Token Hub is independent, and nothing we publish is financial advice.
