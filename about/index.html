About Blockprint

Blockprint reports on crypto markets, protocol engineering, stablecoins, infrastructure, and the policy decisions that move them. Its editorial angle is practical: explain the mechanism, identify the evidence, and separate a meaningful development from a loud narrative.

The site is edited by Mara Ellison, a Chicago-based crypto journalist who covered fintech and payments before spending six years reporting on digital-asset infrastructure, validator economics, and on-chain markets. She has interviewed protocol engineers, exchange operators, and researchers.

Blockprint favors primary documents, clear attribution, and useful context over price-call theatrics. Coverage is written for curious readers who want to understand how crypto systems work and what changes mean in practice.
