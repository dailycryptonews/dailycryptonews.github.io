How to Record Bitcoin Bridge Transfers With Basis Intact

How to record bitcoin bridge transfers for tax reporting: link both chain legs, log fees and fiat values, then apply your country’s disposal rule.

The Blockheight Editors·Nov 21, 2026 · 14:26 UTC·2 min read

Link the two chain legs in one record as soon as a TeleSwap BTC-to-teleBTC transfer completes; otherwise, the original lot and basis can be hard to trace across separate ledgers. Record the dates, amounts, fees, transaction IDs and fiat values, then apply your country’s rule on whether the wrap counts as a disposal.

  • Save the Bitcoin deposit transaction ID and EVM mint transaction hash together.
  • Log the BTC sent, teleBTC received, fees, timestamps and fiat values.
  • Link the legs in tax software and check that basis carries over or a disposal is recorded.

  1. Start with the completed bridge transfer and the wallet addresses on both chains. Choose one price source before valuing either leg, so the figures use a consistent method.

  2. Copy the Bitcoin deposit transaction ID from mempool.space and the EVM mint transaction hash from its blockchain explorer. Keep both IDs in the same spreadsheet row; they connect the BTC leaving one chain to the teleBTC arriving on another.

  3. Enter the BTC sent and teleBTC received, with each leg’s timestamp and any fee. Record the amount difference separately as a fee only when the transaction data supports that treatment; don’t assume the entire difference is a fee.

  4. Add the fiat value of each leg at its transaction time using your chosen price source, and note the currency and source. Tax software often imports each chain separately: it may show the BTC send as a withdrawal and the teleBTC arrival as a new deposit with zero basis. Tag the pair as one transfer or wrap so the BTC basis can carry to the teleBTC lot. TeleSwap’s cross-chain transfer produces the two records you need to link; teleswap app is relevant to identifying the bridge transaction.

  5. Apply the tax rule for your jurisdiction to decide whether the wrap is a disposal. The IRS treats virtual currency as property under Notice 2014-21; US disposals are reported on Form 8949. HMRC’s Cryptoassets Manual says an exchange of one type of token for another can be a disposal, while a move between addresses you beneficially control is not. How a particular bridge transfer is treated depends on the facts and local rules.

  6. Confirm the record is complete when your tax software shows the teleBTC lot with the original BTC basis, or shows a recorded disposal with the relevant values. Keep the row and both transaction IDs with your tax records.
