EU proposal targets crypto networks in 22nd Russia sanctions

The European Commission proposed its 22nd Russia sanctions package on Nov. 23, targeting crypto and payment networks alongside 60 banks, with approval still pending.

The Blockheight Editors·Nov 26, 2026 · 02:59 UTC·2 min read

The European Commission proposed its 22nd Russia sanctions package on Nov. 23, targeting crypto and payment networks alongside 60 Russian banks. President Ursula von der Leyen said the measures are intended to restrict ways Russia finances its war in Ukraine, according to the European Commission statement.

The proposal would extend transaction bans to about 60 banks and remove all of them from SWIFT, the international financial messaging system. The Commission also wants to crack down on crypto and payment networks in Russia and in third countries that help Russia evade sanctions.

Von der Leyen said the EU has launched a Crypto Sanctions Task Force to support that work. The Commission’s statement did not name specific crypto platforms or detail how the proposed restrictions would apply to them.

Other measures in the package include:

  • Listing more than 70 vessels linked to Russia’s shadow fleet, and proposing a ban on tanker vessels insured in Russia.
  • Extending the energy services ban to electricity services, as well as help building, operating or repairing Russian energy infrastructure, including refineries.
  • Banning Russian teams and athletes from competing under the Russian flag at sports events in the EU, with separate proposals to ban official Russian symbols and restrict Russian sponsors’ funding of European sports and cultural associations.

The Commission also proposed visa restrictions on Russian former combatants and a ban on Europeans holding governing posts in companies in Russia. These measures are proposals; the package does not take effect unless all 27 EU member states endorse it.

The approval process can take weeks, and the measures may change before adoption, the Associated Press reported. The Commission has not identified the crypto platforms it intends to target or set out a timeline for the package to enter force.
