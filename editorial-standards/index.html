Editorial standards

Our coverage

Blockheight covers the Crypto industry as news: market structure, protocol changes, regulation, funding and security. Stories describe events and their context, not where prices are headed.

How we source stories

Stories are built on primary and reputable sources: official announcements, filings, court records, on-chain data and established outlets. Where a story relies on a source, it links to it, and it separates what is confirmed from what is still unclear.

Independence

Coverage decisions are made editorially. A mention of a project, token, exchange or service is not an endorsement and not a recommendation to use or buy it.

Corrections

Accuracy matters more than speed. When an error of fact comes to light, the article is corrected.

Not financial advice

Everything on Blockheight is published for general information. It is not financial, investment, legal or tax advice. Crypto assets are volatile; do your own research before making any decision.
