How to Swap Tokens with a Ledger Connected to MetaMask on Ethereum

How to swap tokens with a Ledger connected to MetaMask: add the Ledger account, connect on Matcha swap, then approve and sign each Ethereum transaction on the device.

The Blockheight Editors·Nov 20, 2026 · 11:46 UTC·3 min read

A Ledger account swaps tokens through Matcha swap by connecting through MetaMask and confirming each transaction on the Ledger device, which keeps the private keys on the device.

MetaMask prepares the transaction, but only the Ledger can sign it. For an ERC-20 token sale, that means confirming an approval first and the swap second; selling native ETH needs no token approval.

Complete the Ethereum swap in seven steps

Start with the Ledger plugged in and unlocked, its firmware updated, and ETH in the Ledger account to pay network gas. Have the MetaMask extension installed and the bookmarked Matcha swap URL ready.

  1. Open the Ethereum app on the Ledger. Close Ledger Live before connecting; keep the app open during the swap.
  2. In MetaMask, choose the option to connect a hardware wallet, select Ledger, and pick the account that holds the token and ETH.
  3. Open Matcha swap from your bookmark and connect with MetaMask. Confirm that MetaMask shows the Ledger account you selected.
  4. Choose the token to sell and the token to receive. Use each token’s contract address to identify it, then enter the amount.
  5. Review the quote and minimum received before proceeding. Matcha swap finds a route across liquidity sources; the quote shows the expected exchange and the minimum amount the transaction can deliver. The matcha token list adds background for identifying token names and addresses while you make that choice.
  6. For an ERC-20 sale, submit the token approval. Confirm the request in MetaMask, then read the amount and destination on the Ledger screen and physically confirm there. This approval lets the swap use the specified token.
  7. Submit the swap. Confirm it in MetaMask, then read the transaction details on the Ledger’s own screen before pressing its buttons to sign. Wait for the transaction to finish.
  8. Open the transaction on Etherscan and confirm it shows Success. Then check that the new token balance appears in the Ledger account in MetaMask.

Read the amounts and addresses on the Ledger screen before confirming each request. Malware can change what the computer displays, but the device screen gives you a separate view of the transaction you are signing. Stop if its details do not match the swap you intended.

The Ledger account can complete the swap because MetaMask prepares each transaction and the device signs it. A successful Etherscan result and the updated MetaMask balance show that the swap completed.

Common questions

How do you revoke a token approval from a Ledger account?

Use an Ethereum approval checker or wallet feature to find the token allowance, select the approval to revoke, and submit the revocation transaction. Confirm it in MetaMask and on the Ledger; ETH is needed for gas.

Can the same Ledger account swap on Arbitrum or Polygon through Matcha swap?

The same Ledger can use its account on another supported network, but the network must be selected in MetaMask and the account must hold that network’s native token for gas. Confirm the network and token addresses before swapping.
